Snowball: privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

Snowball keeps your progress on your phone and shows ads from Google AdMob between runs. AdMob collects some data from your device to show and measure those ads. The game itself collects nothing and has no account, server or leaderboard. Remove ads, a one-time purchase, stops the ads.

Ads: Google AdMob

This game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to show an ad between runs. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects and shares data from your device, which may include your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your approximate location worked out from your IP address, how you interact with the ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data. This data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law asks for it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, among others), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and the Privacy options row in the game's Options lets you change that choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment, and the game never sees your card or account details. It learns only whether the purchase was made, so it can stop the ads, and Restore purchases asks Google Play for that again on a new phone.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. It does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard and no chat, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or attribution service of its own.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (the snow you have delivered, what you have built in the village, what you have bought and rolled up) and your settings are saved in the game's own private storage. They never leave the device, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, so AdMob can load ads and Google Play can confirm a purchase.
  • Advertising ID, declared by the AdMob SDK, used for advertising.
  • Vibrate, so the phone can tick when something hits the ground and buzz when a run ends.

The game does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, precise location or files.

Children

The game is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated for everyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected or shared, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in the in-game changelog.
