Candle Gift: privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version

The game itself asks you for nothing and keeps your progress on your phone. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some data from your device to show and measure those ads. Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (the level you have reached, your bank, your best scores, your settings and whether you bought Remove ads) is saved locally on the device, in the game's own private storage. The game does not send it anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard and no chat.

Advertising: Google AdMob

This game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to show ads between levels and, only when you choose it, a rewarded ad on the payout fan. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects and shares data from your device, which may include your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your approximate location worked out from your IP address, how you interact with the ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data. This data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from PRIVACY in the game's pause menu, which appears when your region needs it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Ads use Google AdMob's standard content settings, with alcohol and gambling ads blocked.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details. It only learns from Google Play whether you own Remove ads, so it can turn the ads off and restore them to a new phone through RESTORE PURCHASES.

Permissions

The game uses the internet permission to load ads and talk to Google Play. It does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, precise location or your files.

Children

The game is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected or shared, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in the in-game changelog.
