What leaves your device

Last updated: 13 September 2026 The Lattice collects nothing about you and has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard, no chat and no advertising.

Last updated: 13 September 2026 The Lattice collects nothing about you and has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard, no chat and no advertising. The Lattice is a web game. The Android app is a thin wrapper that shows the game from its website, https://gideon6222.github.io/lattice/, so your device fetches the game's files from that website (hosted by GitHub Pages) the way a browser fetches any page. That is the only network traffic the game makes. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no third-party service of any kind, and nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.
