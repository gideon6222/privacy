Stillwater: privacy policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

The short version

This game collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no network features at all.

What is collected

Nothing.

The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard, no chat, and no advertising.

What leaves your device

Nothing.

The game makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no third-party service of any kind. There is therefore nothing to share with anyone, and no third party receives anything.

What is stored on your device

Your progress — the level you have reached and your best score — is saved locally on the device, in the game's own private storage. It never leaves the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game requests no Android permissions beyond those the operating system grants every app by default. It does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, location, files or the phone's identifiers.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, of any age, and so complies with children's privacy requirements by having nothing to comply with.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in the in-game changelog.
